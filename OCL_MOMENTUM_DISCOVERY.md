## Observed Recurring Characteristics

These visual characteristics repeatedly appear in the examples where
momentum was judged YES. They are observations, not rules.

**Body dominance.** In momentum moves, the candle body occupies most
of the candle's range. Wicks are small relative to the body. The price
commits to a direction within each candle rather than exploring both
sides.

**Consecutive same-direction candles.** Momentum candles come in
unbroken sequences. No opposing candle interrupts the run before the
One Candle. Each candle closes in the same direction as the previous.

**Ground covered.** The move covers visible distance on the chart in
a small number of bars. The price is clearly somewhere different after
the sequence than where it started. The displacement is obvious without
measuring.

**No overlap.** Each candle opens near the prior close and pushes
further. Candle bodies do not overlap significantly with the prior
candle's body. The move stacks rather than oscillates.

**Acceleration or consistency.** The candle bodies are either getting
larger (acceleration) or maintaining a large consistent size. They are
not shrinking. Shrinking bodies appeared only in the BORDERLINE case (#4).

**Absence of compression.** Momentum does not look like a tight range
with small candles. Compression is the opposite signal — candles are
getting smaller and overlapping, which appeared only in the NO case (#9).

**Directional commitment in the very first candle.** The first candle of
a momentum sequence is already large and directional. The move does not
start slowly and build — it begins with intent.

**What was absent in NO / BORDERLINE cases:**

**#9 (NO):** Alternating candle colors, tiny ranges, complete overlap

**#4 (BORDERLINE):** Same direction maintained but with small and
shrinking bodies. The move drifts rather than pushes. No candle makes
a convincing statement on its own. The sequence is directional but lacks
