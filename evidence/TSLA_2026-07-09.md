**V0.3 question raised [DERIVED_INTERPRETATION]:**

Per V0.3, "a material close back through the broken level" can
invalidate the active Market Story. The 10:10 and 10:15 bars close
at 393.98 and 392.43 respectively — well below ORB High (395.54).
The 10:15 bar even reaches 391.91, approaching ORB Low (390.86).

Under a strict reading of V0.3's story invalidation concept, this
deep pullback could constitute a STORY_INVALIDATED event. Price did
not merely dip through the broken level — it closed 3+ points below
it for multiple bars.

Yet Max identified this session as a valid LONG entry. He described
the full-day outcome where price broke ORB High, PMH, and PDH, and
he identified the retest of PMH as his entry.

**This is not recorded as a contradiction** because V0.3 explicitly
marks the "material re-entry" threshold as an open question. But
this session provides important evidence for defining that threshold:
Max considered the LONG story valid despite a 3-point, multi-bar
pullback below the ORB break level.
