## Key Principles Extracted

These are new concepts Max expressed while reading this session,
documented here as evidence. They are not yet part of V0.3.

### 1. Re-entry into ORB after break = break not confirmed

When candles close back inside the ORB range after a break, the
break is not confirmed. No displacement means the break does not
exist yet. The Max Bot returns to standby and waits for a new
setup in either direction. [MAX_LABEL]

**Important nuance [MAX_LABEL]:** A single candle re-entering the
zone after displacement does NOT automatically invalidate the break.
After displacement is confirmed, a candle entering the zone is the
retest the Max Bot was waiting for. Before displacement, re-entry
means the break is still unclassified.

### 2. Only strong marked levels

Max does not trade on intraday swing highs or lows. He only trades
on pre-marked levels: ORB High/Low, PMH/PML, PDH/PDL, and OCL
formed during the session. The 10:05 higher high being retested at
12:30 was "perfect" but not taken because it was not a marked
level. [MAX_LABEL]

### 3. "Too late" is a valid reason to skip

Even when valid OCL retests finally appear (13:05 → 13:30), Max
does not enter because the market has already moved too far. The
Max Bot must have a concept of "too late." [MAX_LABEL]

### 4. After acceleration, OCL is the only level left

When the market breaks all structural levels (ORB, PMH, PDH), the
only levels remaining are previous highs or immediate order blocks
(OCL). The OCL exists by necessity — it is the only level available
when structural levels are all behind. [MAX_LABEL]

### 5. NO TRADE is a frequent, valid result

This session produced no entry. Three break attempts, two failures,
one success too late. The Max Bot must accept zero trades as a
normal outcome. [MAX_LABEL]
